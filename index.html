01 DaulatLabs

Software for the shops that keep a neighbourhood running.

DaulatLabs makes systems for restaurants, clinics, insurance agencies and residential communities. A business starts with one, and the rest plug into the same foundation when it is ready.

Founder-led, based in Kuala Lumpur. Production is truth: we do not sell what is not real.

    05 About me

    I like problems you can walk up to.

    Door readers, front desks, kitchens, clinics. That is where software meets the real world, and where I like to work.

    • i.Start from the support queue. Real tickets say more than opinions.
    • ii.Write it down: specs, user stories, acceptance criteria.
    • iii.Data before arguments. SQL and Power BI every week.
    • iv.Ship small, then check it in production.
